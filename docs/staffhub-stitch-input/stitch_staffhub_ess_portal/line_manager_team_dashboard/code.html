Supervisory Confidentiality Standard & Privacy Boundary

In accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019) and Corporate Policy Sec 2.1, individual remuneration records (gross salaries, statutory PAYE deductions, NSSF remittances, bank accounts) and National Identification Numbers (NIN) are strictly segregated from line supervisory consoles. Line management authority is strictly operational, encompassing duty scheduling, attendance confirmation, and leave concurrence.
